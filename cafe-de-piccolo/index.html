About Us

Italian flavours on Madhya Marg

Café De Piccolo brings Italian cafe classics to Madhya Marg in Sector 9-D, Chandigarh — from vodka-sauce pasta and baked chicken lasagna to thin-crust pizzas fresh from the oven.

Start the day with an English breakfast, or finish on a sweet note with banoffee pie and Nutella cheesecake, paired with speciality coffee or a mug of hot chocolate. We are open every day from 11 AM to 10:30 PM.
