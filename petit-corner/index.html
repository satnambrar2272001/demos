About Petit Corner

Built for the morning rush, steps from Flinders Street Station

Petit Corner opens at 6:30 AM on Flinders Street, made for Melbourne's morning commuters — and food blog Smooch Food called it "the best food quality of our Melbourne café stops so far".

Toasties pressed to order, fresh baguettes, French pastries and lattes with a twist — hojicha with salted cream, salted honey foam, and matcha with peach cream.
