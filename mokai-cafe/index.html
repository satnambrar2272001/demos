About Mokai

Mokai Cafe sits on Dr Ambedkar Road in Pali Hill, Bandra West. The menu leans Asian-fusion — Japanese, Thai and Singaporean influences — with ceremonial-grade matcha and hand-crafted sandos at its centre.

Open every day from 8:00 in the morning to 11:30 at night, it is an easy stop for a slow breakfast, a working lunch, or a late-evening coffee.
