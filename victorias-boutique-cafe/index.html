About Us

A boutique cafe in the Inner Market, Sector 8

Victoria's Boutique Cafe is a cosy corner of Sector 8-B's Inner Market, Chandigarh, built around speciality coffee — from a smooth cortado or cappuccino to our own Victoria blend.

Pair your cup with avocado toast, a cottage cheese focaccia sandwich, golden waffles or a slice of homemade cake. We open bright and early on weekdays, stay open till midnight on Saturdays, and welcome you in from lunchtime on Sundays.
