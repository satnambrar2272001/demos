A Dallas Favorite

Tucked into Brockbank Drive, Restaurant Y Tacos El Chino serves the kind of Mexican cooking that keeps neighbors coming back — traditional tacos, rich soups and breakfast plates, made fresh from morning to night. With a perfect 5.0-star rating, it is one of Dallas's best-loved taquerias.
