About La Luna

Your local beachside stop on Henley Beach Road

La Luna Cafe is a small, friendly beachside cafe in Henley Beach South, pouring great coffee from early morning — we open at 6 AM on weekdays, perfect for a pre-beach or pre-work stop.

Locals know us for our almond lattes, macchiatos and the bacon & egg brioche. Whether it is a fresh juice after a beach walk or a homemade soup at lunch, everything is made fresh to order.
