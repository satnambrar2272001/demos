About Shelter

Shelter Cafe x Coffee by Javaphile is tucked into Noorie House on Sherly Rajan Road, just off Carter Road in Bandra West — an easy-going spot built around good coffee.

From iced cortados to the signature hot chocolate, with sourdough pizzas and smoothie bowls served through the day, it's open daily from 8:00 in the morning to 11:00 at night.
