About Us

A Paddington favourite on Given Terrace

Inch Hair Dressing is a boutique salon on Given Terrace in Paddington, loved for its colour work — from full and 3/4-head foils to correction and refresh appointments — and for cuts that truly suit the hair they're cut on.

Curly hair is cut with the curl pattern in mind, precision cuts are tailored to the individual, and keratin smoothing treatments keep hair glossy and manageable. Open Tuesday to Saturday, with late nights on Wednesday and Thursday.
