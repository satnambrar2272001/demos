About Us

Level 3, 29 Elizabeth Street — in the heart of Hobart's CBD

Red Salon is a Hobart CBD hair studio known for its blonde specialist work and seamless balayage, using Wella colour for rich, lasting results.

From precision style cutting and keratin smoothing to extensions, perms, wedding styling and makeup, the team looks after the whole look under one roof — open six days a week, Monday to Saturday.
