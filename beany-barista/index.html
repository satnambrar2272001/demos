About Beany Barista

Beany Barista is a cosy neighbourhood coffee stop on Pakhowal Road in Sant Ishar Singh Nagar, Ludhiana — the kind of place where the coffee is poured fresh and the brownies come out fudgy.

The counter serves caramel and hazelnut lattes, iced Americanos, frappes and mochas, with walnut brownies, grilled sandwiches, paninis and thick milkshakes alongside. Open every day, 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
