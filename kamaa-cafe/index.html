About Us

A Sector 7 favourite on Madhya Marg

Kamaa Cafe sits on Madhya Marg in Sector 7-C, Chandigarh — a lively spot known for its signature mocktails and cocktails, comforting bowls of creamy truffle soup, and flavour-packed plates like Achari chicken.

Whether you are dropping in for gourmet sandwiches and spring rolls with friends or settling in for a relaxed evening, Kamaa is open every day from 11 in the morning to 11 at night.
