About Alley Brew

A hidden-gem coffee bar at 21 Bank Street

Alley Brew Co. is Adelaide's alleyway specialty coffee bar — small, creative and serious about flavour. Glam Adelaide scored our flat white 9.1 out of 10, the highest score in its Adelaide best-flat-white rundown.

Alongside classic specialty coffee, we are known for playful signature drinks: sweet-salted cream cold brew, strawberry matcha and a mocha with a raspberry candy twist.
