About Pilgrim

Specialty coffee in the heart of Hobart

Pilgrim Coffee is a Hobart institution — the team proudly calls itself the market leader of specialty coffee in Tasmania, and hundreds of five-star reviews suggest the locals agree.

Inside, warm timber and church-bench seating make it one of the cosiest rooms in the city. Come for a slow filter brew and one of the cinnamon rolls reviewers can't stop talking about.
