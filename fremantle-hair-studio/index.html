About Us

Inside Fremantle Malls, in the heart of Freo

Fremantle Hair Studio sits inside Fremantle Malls on William Street — a bright, welcoming salon best known for its weft and bond/KTI hair extensions, the studio's speciality.

From custom colour and colour correction to scalp bleach, blonde & balayage and fresh haircuts, the team is open Wednesday to Sunday, 10 AM to 5 PM. Walk-ins are welcome to drop by, or call ahead to book your chair.
